I am a Project Assistant Professor in the Echizen Laboratory at the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Tokyo, Japan. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Monash University and my B.S. in Electronic Information Engineering from Northeastern University, China. Before entering academia, I worked in the IT industry and held a director-level position.

My research focuses on AI security and privacy, spanning adversarial machine learning, security of emerging AI systems, and AI evaluation and governance.
